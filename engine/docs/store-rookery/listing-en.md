## App name (30)
Rookery

## Short description (80)
Direct a team of AIs to research, write, build apps and make videos for you.

## Full description (4000)
Rookery isn't an AI that answers alone — it's an AI office that directs multiple generative AIs.
Tell it what you want done: it looks up what needs looking up, and hands longer jobs to AI workers as tasks.

■ What it does
· Analyze documents and YouTube videos — summaries, key claims, numbers
· Competitor analysis and market research
· Games, apps and small web tools
· About 60-second explainer videos (script, voice, subtitles)

■ A preview beside every result
Results land in the preview panel beside the chat, stacked by version. You can restore an earlier version anytime.

■ It gets better as you review
Approve a result or request changes. Before starting, each worker writes down a prediction; your verdict is compared against it and the worker adjusts its own standards. Request changes and it fixes the work exactly as you described.

■ Continue from computer to phone
In Rookery on your computer, choose "Continue on phone" in the menu and scan the QR code — the same account opens on your phone, no password needed.

■ See what you spend
Your usage is always visible, and every company has a limit.

■ Your language
Rookery follows your phone's language (English, Japanese, Korean) and replies in the language you write in. You can switch the language in the menu.

Try it a few times without signing in. Sign in to save your chats and results.
If an AI answer or result is inappropriate, tell us with "Report" in the app.
Delete your account and all data anytime with "Delete account" in the menu.
