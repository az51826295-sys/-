Ashen Expedition does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. There is no sign-up or login.

Game progress (floors, members, materials, gear, etc.) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to our servers, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
